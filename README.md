This game is a 2D rogue-like dungeon crawler with turn based combat. You as a hero has to delve deeper and deeper into the dungeons, caves and other structures to find the wicked witch that threatens the city and kill it. Along the way you have to defeat various monsters big and small. You can search for treasure which help you gear up for the challenges you face. You have to avoid traps and carefully choose your battles. Meanwhile you level up and can select from talents presented. The battles are turn based and you have to manage your resources to turn the tide in your favor.
